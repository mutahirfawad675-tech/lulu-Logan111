YOUR NETWORK IS YOUR NET WORTH

The phrase "Your network is your net worth" is widely attributed to Porter Gale, a marketing expert and author. She popularized the concept in her 2013 book titled Your Network Is Your Net Worth, where she emphasizes the importance of building relationships and leveraging connections for personal and professional success.

Although the idea of networking being crucial for success has been around for a long time, Gale's book helped bring this specific phrase into common usage.
