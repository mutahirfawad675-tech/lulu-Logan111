We would both like to express our sincere appreciation for Lulu Logan — not only an exceptional real estate professional, but also a truly remarkable person. Our journey with Lulu began when we spotted a listing online and needed an agent representative to view the home. Zillow connected us with Lulu, and after an initial hour-long coffee meeting, we had no idea how significant her role would become over the next ten months. From the outset, Lulu impressed us with her experience and professionalism, but what really stood out was her thoughtful approach. She took time to understand our preferences, financial parameters, desired neighborhoods, and — most importantly — our reasons for house hunting. With every showing, she gathered deeper insights into our evolving tastes, needs, and even areas of disagreement, always with patience and precision. As we were combining two households into one, Lulu asked to visit our individual homes. What seemed like a simple step turned out to be pivotal: it gave her a clear picture of our lifestyles and helped guide us to a home that would truly suit both of us. Her ability to understand not just real estate, but the people involved, became a key factor in finding the right fit. In the midst of our search, I also decided to sell a condo I owned in Southern California. Lulu didn’t hesitate — she took on what became a very complex and drawn-out process, filled with challenges including repairs, upgrades, a difficult HOA, and long-distance coordination. Through it all, Lulu was unwavering. She managed every aspect with remarkable diligence and professionalism. That sale, which might have discouraged many agents, closed successfully thanks to her persistence and problem-solving. Then, two months ago, we found the home — a perfect fit that captured both of us immediately. The home was at the top of our price range, but Lulu found a creative way to structure the deal and make it work. Once again, she navigated a complicated purchase process and got us to the finish line. Over the course of nearly a year, Lulu earned our complete trust and respect. Her vast knowledge — not only of real estate but also of finance and estate planning — is only surpassed by her people skills. She’s a master at managing personalities, expectations, and emotions, all while keeping the process on track. Lulu isn’t just a phenomenal agent — she’s become a friend. We wholeheartedly recommend her to anyone buying or selling a home. With Lulu Logan, you are truly in the best hands.
